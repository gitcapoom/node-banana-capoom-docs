# AI Quickstart

The AI Quickstart feature lets you generate workflows from natural language descriptions or preset templates.

For full documentation, see the [upstream AI Quickstart guide](https://node-banana-docs.vercel.app/guides/ai-quickstart).

## Quick Summary

1. Open the Quickstart panel from the header
2. Choose a **preset template** or describe your workflow in plain English
3. Review the generated nodes and connections
4. Adjust prompts and settings as needed
5. Run the workflow

## Preset Templates

- Product Shot
- Model + Product
- Color Variations
- Background Swap
- Style Transfer
- Scene Composite

## Tips

- Be specific in descriptions: include inputs, transformations, and expected outputs
- Start with presets and modify them for best results
- Review generated prompts before running — they may need tuning for your use case
