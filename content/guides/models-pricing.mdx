# Models & Pricing

For complete model and pricing details, see the [upstream Models & Pricing guide](https://node-banana-docs.vercel.app/guides/models-pricing).

## Quick Reference

### Image Generation

| Model | Provider | Resolution | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| nano-banana | Gemini | 1K | ~$0.039 |
| nano-banana-pro | Gemini | 1K-4K | $0.134-$0.240 |
| Various | Replicate | Varies | Per-model pricing |
| Various | fal.ai | Varies | Per-model pricing |

### Video Generation

Available through Replicate and fal.ai. Typically takes minutes rather than seconds.

### 3D Generation

Available through fal.ai. Generates GLB format models.

### Text Generation

| Model | Provider | Notes |

| GPT-4.1 Mini/Nano | OpenAI | Alternative option |
